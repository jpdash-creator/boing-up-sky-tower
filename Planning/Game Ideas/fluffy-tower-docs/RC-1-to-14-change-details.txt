RC 9: CAP REPLAY FARMING BELOW 3 STARS
======================================================================
Problem: replaying a level with fewer than 3 stars pays full coins every time, no limit.
At about 17.6 levels an hour:

Case                 Coins per replay   Coins per hour   Share of first-clear income (4,650/h)
1-star replay             130              2,290             49%
2-star replay             190              3,344             72%
3-star replay             125              2,200             47%
So the coin faucet never dries up.

Proposal: full pay only when the run raises the level's stars. Every other replay pays 50%.
Case                                Before   After
Replay raising 1 -> 2 stars          190      190
2-star replay, no improvement        190       95
1-star replay, no improvement        130       65
Files: CoinPayoutRules.IsReducedReplay(starsBefore) becomes "has cleared before and this run's
stars <= previous stars"; Rewards.cs lines 108-118; Tests/Simulation/GemCoinEconomyTests.cs
and RewardsTests; doc 22 point 6.
Check first: gem pickups on replays. Doc 26 says cleared levels strip their pickups, so keep
gems as they are. Effort: about 60 min. This is a rule change, so the owner decides.
