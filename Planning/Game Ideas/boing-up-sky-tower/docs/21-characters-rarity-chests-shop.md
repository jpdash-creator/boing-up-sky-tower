# 21. Characters by rarity, chests and shop (owner issue list 24, 2026-10-24)

Extends docs/20.

## Characters
- Rarity: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary. Box colours with a seam (border): Common light grey with white, Uncommon light green with white, Rare blue with white, Epic purple with gold, Legendary yellow gold with gold.
- Momo is the Common character of region 1, Capi the forest girl of region 2 (the Sporeling was deleted 2026-11-01; old saves that own or selected it fall back to Momo). A region unlock gives its Common character (owned without a save entry). Common characters have no power.
- **Region heroes (owner issue list 28, item 3, 2026-11-01):** every region has exactly one designated Common hero: R1 Momo (always unlocked), R2 Capi (forest girl; replaces the deleted Sporeling); planned R3 beach hero, R4 cute candy hero, R5 cute furry Shaolin monk (art comes later). A hero is HIDDEN (Heroes screen, selection, any list) until its region is UNLOCKED on the map. The first time that happens, a one-time flash pop-up "New hero joins you!" (hero picture, name, rarity Common, one OK button) shows on the next home or map screen; only after OK is the hero on the Heroes screen. The reveal is saved at once (`heroesRevealed` id list, additive save field, no version bump), so it shows once. An existing save whose region is already unlocked but not yet revealed gets the pop-up once (Momo never). If the chosen character is a hidden hero the game falls back to Momo. Region heroes never drop from chests. The pop-up uses the card_yellow panel from Art/Rewards; the single swap point for the OpenArt background is `Resources/Art/Ui/hero_reveal_bg` (`HeroRevealCard.BackgroundResource`). Only motion: a 0.25 s pop-in, skipped with reduced motion; no flashing, no confetti (CUX-6). **Adding a hero** needs one `CharacterSpec` row in `CharacterDefs.All` (Region = its RegionIndex) plus the art in `Resources/Characters/<id>/`; `CharacterCatalog` is built from that list.
- Uncommon and above come only from chests and the shop (gems). They have powers: Uncommon 1, Rare 2, Elite 3, Legendary 4 (any of Flight, Wall Stick, Magnet, Double Jump, and the other power-ups). A power is ready at the start of a run and, after it ran, has a cooldown: Uncommon 30 s, Rare 22 s, Elite 15 s, Legendary 10 s. (Data model done in Economy/Characters.cs; the in-run mechanic comes with the first Uncommon+ character, none exists yet.)
- A duplicate character is just another copy (Bug List 5 F3 removed the 25 percent gem refund; see doc 26).
- Character screen: the chosen character on the green land stage on top; below, a scrolling list, 5 per row, of rarity boxes. Owned in colour (tap to choose, tick on the chosen one), others dark.
- **Characters are Coming Soon in the shop and in chests** (switched off in code, doc 22).

## Chests (gems only; open 1 or 10) - current numbers
- **Iron Chest**: 100 gems (open 10: 1,000). Contains 1 power-up and 400 to 600 coins.
- **Gold Chest**: 300 gems (open 10: 3,000). Contains 3 power-ups and 1,000 to 1,400 coins.
- Each power-up is one of the 7 types with equal chance. No characters until they launch. No chests are earned from play (doc 22). Average value and return: doc 27 section 10.
- (The first version of this file listed 3 power-ups and 800 to 1,000 coins for Iron, 6 power-ups and 2,500 to 3,000 coins for Golden; replaced by doc 22.)

## Shop
- Five tabs: **Bundles, Chests, Power-ups, Coins, Gems**, plus a Subscription button (doc 26). Characters are a Coming soon card.
- Popular / Best value badges: smaller font, centred on the card top.
- VIP left the shop: it became the Subscription (Weekly and Monthly, doc 26); there are no VIP tiers.

## Hero sheet (owner 2026-11-01)
Screen `Hero` (`HeroSheetScreen.cs`, opened by `MetaController.ShowHeroSheet`), art `Art/_candidates/hero_sheet/sheet_a_wood.png` (owner pick, Layout A) cut by `Tools/art_pipeline/process_hero_sheet.py` into `Assets/Resources/Art/HeroSheet/` (plates plus `slices.json`, importer rule in `ArtImportPostprocessor`, version 12). If a plate file is missing the screen falls back to plain rounded wood/gold colour plates.
- **Opened by:** (1) the (i) button at the RIGHT of the shown hero on the Heroes screen (`CharactersScreen`, button `HeroInfo`, at least 48 dp, A11y label "Hero details"), (2) a hero tile on the Heroes screen (a tile tap no longer chooses the hero; the sheet has the "Use this hero" button, "In use" when it is the chosen one). **The home screen has no (i) button and the home hero is not tappable** (owner 2026-11-02).
- **Header (fixed):** back on the left socket (returns to where it was opened), close on the right socket (home); both real buttons of at least 48 dp.
- **Top block:** the hero (rig figure) in the portrait frame; name plate, rarity banner (grey ribbon tinted by rarity: Common grey, Uncommon green, Rare blue, Epic purple, Legendary gold, with the rarity word in ink or white, whichever reads at 4.5:1) and region plate on its right.
- **Tab Stats:** six stats in a 3 x 2 table (icon square + label + plain number: Jump, Speed, Control, Wall Bounce, Coins +N, Gems +N, from `CharacterStats.Sheet`; numbers and formulas: docs/44-power-mechanics-bible.md sections 3 and 7b) and the Use button. **Tab Details:** derived panel with three rows (Full-speed jump, Stopping distance, Time to top speed, from the same sheet) and the hero power panel (Common: "No special power"; otherwise the power names and the cooldown by rarity).
- **Typography and layout (owner 2026-11-02, sleek):** small centred text, nothing bigger than needed. Header title 48 px, Back/Close visuals 126 px (the 48 dp minimum), tabs 44 px, name 46, rarity 44, region 42; stat icons 88 px (about 70 percent of before); stat label (at the 16 sp floor) above the value (48), both centred in the plate with 34 px padding from its border; Details rows (label at the floor, value 46) and the power panel (heading 46, body 42) centred too. The spare height of tall screens is shared out (`HeroSheetScreen.Plan`: slightly bigger portrait up to 48 percent of the width, taller stat cells/rows, larger gaps, then space above the first block) so the screen is not top-heavy; with no spare height (short screen, 130 percent text) the base sizes stay and it scrolls.
- **Text:** drawn in code on the plates, best fit from the base size down to the 16 sp floor, text scale applied; the content scrolls inside the screen only when it does not fit (header stays). Tests: `CriticalUxTests` (also: no `HeroInfo`/`HeroTap` on home, `HeroInfo` on the Heroes screen opens the sheet) screens `hero-sheet-common`, `-uncommon`, `-rare`, `-epic`, `-legendary` (fake heroes via `HeroSheetData.From`), each also on the Details tab; `HeroSheetTests` (EditMode) for data, plates, buttons.
