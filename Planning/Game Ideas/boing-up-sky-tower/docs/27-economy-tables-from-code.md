## 1. Rate card and dollar columns

**Fixed conversion [DOC 22, CODE]: 1 gem = 20 coins. 1 coin = 0.05 gem.** The coin packs in section 4.3 pay 20 coins per gem for the four smallest packs and up to 25 coins per gem for the largest (a volume bonus). Exchange of the real money: $1 = 95 rupees (doc 22).

Every table shows **gems, coins and dollars**. A gem is worth different dollars depending on the pack it came from, so three dollar columns are used (list prices, promo off, as the game ships):

## 2. Findings in one page

1. **Rewards, not play, are the gem faucet.** Gem pickups give about 260 gems over the whole 5-region campaign at 60 percent pickup (413 at 100 percent). The Main rewards give 580 gems, the Daily rewards up to 45 gems **every day**, the Weekly rewards 160 gems a week. A player who finishes every daily and weekly task earns about **68 gems a day (2,040 a month, $22 at ref)** plus 1,500+ coins a day and one random power-up a day.
2. **The daily random power-up alone is worth 60.7 gems ($0.67) a day**, 1,821 gems a month. It is the biggest single free item (doc 22 open item 2).
3. **The subscription is the cheapest gem source by far.** Monthly $4.99 pays 500 gems + 18,000 coins (1,400 GE; 1,764 with the 6 power-ups) = **281 to 354 GE per dollar**, against 63 to 125 gems per dollar in the packs. Weekly $1.49 pays 345 GE = 232 GE per dollar.
4. **Power-ups are expensive in gems and cheap in coins.** 50 or 75 gems is a whole region of gem pickups (51 to 54 gems), but 1,000 or 1,500 coins is only 4 to 6 levels of coin income (250 coins a level). Free players buy power-ups with coins; gems go to revives, chests, bundles and skips.
5. **Price per second is uneven.** Rocket costs 18.75 gems per second of effect and Flight 7.5, against 0.83 for Shield and 1.67 for Double Jump, Wall Stick and Magnet.
6. **Chests are a gem sink with a deliberate loss:** Iron returns 85.7 percent of its price in value, Gold 80.7 percent. Bundles are the opposite: 1.41 to 1.79 times their price (average 1.60). Direct power-up purchase is 1.0 times.
7. **Gems are the hub currency.** Every shop item except the two subscriptions and the gem packs is bought with gems or coins. The only money products are the 9 gem packs and 2 subscriptions.
8. **Ads are only the revive ad today** (first revive of a run, free). One ad saves 5 gems ($0.055 at ref) and earns the developer about $0.01. Everything else on the ad plan (double result, free chest, daily power-up) is not built.
9. **Several config values were stale (fixed in RC 1, 2026-11-01: `coinsPerGem` is now 20 in `remote_config.json`, the manifest and the test; `vipGemsPerDay` and the unread per-level `replayCoinPercent` were deleted): the docs still described gear, VIP tiers, 4-tab shops and old packs. Section 3 lists them all.
10. **Characters are only two commons** (Momo, Sporeling, both free). The character shop is "coming soon"; chests drop no characters (switched off). Doc 22 notes that when switched on, a character per chest would return 276 to 286 percent of the chest price.

## 7. Power-ups

- 7 types are sold; stock per type, no cap, no carry cap, no loadout limit in code (rework 2026-10-31); a tap while a power-up is active **adds** its full duration.

## 10. Treasure chests [CODE, DOC 22]

### 10.1 Rules

- Two chests, **Iron** and **Gold**, paid in **gems only**, open 1 or open 10 (10 times the price, no discount).
- Iron: 1 power-up + 400 to 600 coins. Gold: 3 power-ups + 1,000 to 1,400 coins. Each power-up is one of the 7 types with equal chance. Characters switched off.
- A 50-gem confirm applies; the result shows coins and power-ups; saved once per open. Seeded roll (open index), recomputable by a server.
- No chests are earned from play, rewards or subscription.

Pull toward buying is strong only for the **gems-only** sinks (chests, bundles, skips, revives). Power-ups and coins can be reached with free coins, which the rewards supply in large amounts (52,740 coins a month for a daily player).

### 16.3 Revenue model per daily active player [ASSUMPTION]

Money products: gem packs and subscriptions. IAP gross = 2 percent x $18.40 / 30 / 0.20 = **$0.061 per DAU**, net of the 15 percent fee **$0.052**.

| Scenario | Ads (revive only) | IAP net | Total net | Ads share |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Low (eCPM $4, 0.7 views) | $0.003 | $0.052 | $0.055 | 5% |
| Base (eCPM $10, 0.7 views) | $0.007 | $0.052 | **$0.059** | 12% |
| Base with 2.5 views (if double result etc. were built) | $0.025 | $0.052 | $0.077 | 32% |
| High (eCPM $25, 2.5 views) | $0.063 | $0.052 | $0.115 | 55% |

Payer mix guess (per 100 monthly payers, $1,840 gross): gem packs about 50 percent, subscriptions about 35 percent, rest in packs of the smaller sizes; whales (8 percent of payers) about 52 percent of revenue. These are guesses to be replaced by soft-launch data.

### 16.4 Guardrails for ads and gems

1. Rewarded ads must not pay more gems than the revive saves (5 gems) per view.
2. Any future ad gem reward needs a daily cap; the free gem flow is already 45 gems a day.
3. Retry stays one tap; ads are optional (CUX-7).
4. The ad key and unit id are placeholders (`AdConfig`); release has no ads until the owner supplies them.
