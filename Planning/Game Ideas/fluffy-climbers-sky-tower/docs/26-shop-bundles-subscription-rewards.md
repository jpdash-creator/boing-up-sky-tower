- **Chests with a hero card (Bug List 5 F3, supersedes the contents text of the bullet above).** A chest holds coins + power-ups + EXACTLY ONE hero card, like a bundle (docs/37). Prices unchanged (Iron 100 / 1,000, Gold 300 / 3,000 gems for Open 1 / Open 10, no discount). Final contents per chest:

  | Chest | Gems | Coins | Power-ups | Hero card odds | Expected card (gems) | Worth |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | Iron | 100 | 800 to 1,200 (was 400 to 600) | 1 random | 70 Common, 20 Uncommon, 10 Rare | 73 | **x1.8** |
  | Gold | 300 | 3,000 to 4,200 (was 1,000 to 1,400) | 3 random | 50 Uncommon, 40 Rare, 10 Elite (code `Epic`) | 210 | **x1.9** |

  Worth = (average coins / 20 + power-ups x 60.71 gems (the mean gem price of the 7 implemented types) + expected card gems) / Open 1 gems. Iron: (50 + 60.71 + 73) / 100 = 1.84. Gold: (180 + 182.14 + 210) / 300 = 1.91. Even the worst coin roll with the cheapest power-ups (1.63 / 1.70) and the best (2.08 / 2.15) stay inside 1.5 to 2.5. Open 10 is ten openings for ten times the gems, so the same worth. The coins were raised because the old chests were a deliberate loss (about 0.8x); with a hero card inside they must be worth 1.5 to 2.5 like bundles. Coins are valued at the shop rate (20 per gem).
  - **Card gem values: ONE table.** `ChestRolls.CardGems(rarity)` reads `BundleCards.TierGems` for Uncommon, Rare, Elite (100 / 250 / 600, so a chest and a bundle value a card identically) and adds `ChestRolls.CommonCardGems = 40` for a Common card (3 Commons merge into an Uncommon: 3 x 40 = 120, a little above one Uncommon). These are WORTH values; the Heroes shop retail prices (`HeroPurchase.Prices` 150 / 300 / 700 / 1,500) are higher on purpose (a chosen tier at a known price against a random card). Change the numbers in those two places only.
  - **The draw** (`Economy/ChestCards.cs`, pure, same style as `BundleCards`): tier by the odds above, then a hero uniformly among the heroes of that tier whose REGION IS OPEN (the bundle predicate `IsBundleRegionOpen`: region 1 always, others when unlocked). Common heroes = the base hero of each open region (never a merge tier). Uncommon and up = merge tiers of the lines of open regions (`BundleCards.Pool`). A tier with nobody in an open region falls back to the next LOWER tier that has a hero (Rare and Elite do not exist yet, so today they give Uncommon, and an Iron Rare hit gives Uncommon too); if nothing exists at or below, the lowest tier above. Deterministic: the roll comes from `Rng(SeedOf(kind, openIndex, accountSeed))` with the per-account seed `Data.Bundles.Seed` and the running chest counter `Data.ChestOpens`, never `UnityEngine.Random`.
  - **Duplicates are not refunded any more.** A hero the player already has is simply another copy (another card of the same hero on the Heroes screen, docs/34 section 10 and docs/36). `ChestRolls.DuplicateRefundGems`, `DuplicateRefundPercent`, `CharactersEnabled` and `ChestContents.Characters/Duplicate/Gems` were removed. `TryOpenChests` pays the gems, adds coins and power-ups, then adds exactly one copy per chest through `AddCopies` (which saves; the first save also holds everything else).
  - **UI:** each card has the 48 dp "i" (top left), the name, and the italic "Worth x1.8" (top right, the name's font, no star, no pill), the chest picture on the left, and on the right icon chips (coin range, power-ups "x1" / "x3" with the bolt icon, the hero card as the `HeroCardIcon` with "?") above Open 1 / Open 10. The "i" popup (`TreasureChestScreen.ShowInfo`): the chips, "Only heroes of your open regions (N)", the three odds columns in rarity colours, "Worth x1.8 for 100 gems", "Same hero twice? You keep both.". The confirm popup says the worth and "Includes 1 hero card (?)". The reveal (`ChestReveal`) is compact: small chest (tap skips), icon chips for coins and each power-up with counts, then one small row per hero (portrait, name, rarity word in its colour, "x2" when repeated; a scrolling list after four) and Collect.
