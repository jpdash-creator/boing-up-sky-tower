# Fluffy Climbers: Tower Jump

Fluffy Climbers: Tower Jump is a 2D vertical-jumping arcade game for mobile. Climb an endless tower of platforms with Momo and friends, collect stars and gems, and unlock new regions. Free to play, with optional purchases and optional rewarded ads.
