# Fluffy Climbers: Tower Jump Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-09

> **In short**
>
> - Fluffy Climbers: Tower Jump ("the Game") has no account system of its own, no server of its own, no analytics or crash-reporting software, no chat and no push notifications.
> - Your progress (levels, stars, coins, gems, heroes, power-ups, records and settings) is stored on your device. If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, a copy of your progress is also stored in your Google account through Google's Saved Games service. Guests keep their progress on the device only.

> - The Game shows optional rewarded video ads, and only after you tap an ad button. The advertising software built into the Game (Unity LevelPlay and the ad networks it mediates) uses your device's advertising ID. Ads are requested as non-personalised unless you have given consent where a consent prompt is shown.
> - You can play as a guest, unlink your Google account, reset all progress, and reset or delete your advertising ID at any time.
> - The Game is not directed to children under 13. If you are under 18, a parent or guardian must agree to this policy and to our Terms of Use for you. See section 15.

## 1. Who we are

Fluffy Climbers: Tower Jump ("the Game", "we", "us", "our") is a 2D vertical-jumping arcade game for mobile devices published by J. P. Dash, an individual developer based in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India. The Game is distributed on Google Play under the application id `com.jdlabs.fluffyclimbers` and may later be distributed on the Apple App Store.

For privacy questions, requests and complaints, contact jpdash.creator@gmail.com. J. P. Dash is the controller (under the GDPR and the UK GDPR), the business (under the CCPA/CPRA), and the data fiduciary and grievance officer (under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023) for the limited personal data described in this policy.

## 2. Scope and how to read this policy

This policy explains what data the Game handles, what stays on your device, what third parties receive through the software built into the Game, and the choices and rights you have. It applies to the Game on every platform where we offer it, to its testing and pre-release builds, and to e-mail you send us. It does not cover the services of Google, Apple, Unity or ad networks; their own policies are linked where they apply. Your use of the Game is governed by our Terms of Use, published next to this policy.

Where this policy says "we receive" or "we collect", it means that data reaches the developer. Most of the data described below is processed only on your device, or only by the third-party service named, and never reaches us. We do not operate a server of our own and we have no database of players.

## 3. Data stored on your device

The Game keeps the following in its private app storage on your device:

- **Progress:** levels cleared, stars earned, best scores, clear counts, timed records and medals per level and per gameplay mode, best floor and records in endless and time-attack modes, unlocked regions, and which hero introductions you have seen.
- **Wallet and items:** coins, gems, owned power-ups, owned heroes and their copies, chest-opening counter, bundle offers and their timers, reward claims and daily or weekly counters, and subscription claim state (days left, last claim day).
- **Purchase records:** for each real-money purchase, the product id, the kind of product (gem pack or subscription), the store transaction id and the time. These records let the Game restore your subscription days and prevent a one-time bonus from being granted twice. They contain no payment details.
- **Account fields:** whether you chose guest or Google play, the Google Play Games player id of a linked account, the time of the last cloud sync, the time of the last change, and when the "connect Google" reminder was last shown.
- **Settings:** sound, music, haptics, reduced motion, left-handed layout, text size, gameplay difficulty, pace ghost, and promotional flags. Development-only test switches exist in development builds and have no effect in release builds.

This data is written as plain (unencrypted) files in the app's private folder, which other apps cannot read on an unmodified device. The Game is configured to opt out of Android device backup, so the save is not copied into Google device backups or device-to-device transfers; the only off-device copy is the optional cloud save described in section 4. Nothing in this section is sent to us. Uninstalling the Game deletes it.

## 4. Google Play Games sign-in and cloud save (optional)

On first launch you choose **Sign in with Google Play** or **Play as guest**. You can change your choice later in Settings. Signing in is never required to play.

**What the Game accesses.** When you sign in, the Game uses the Google Play Games Services software on your device and receives two things from it: your Play Games **player id** (an identifier Google creates for you for this Game only) and your Play Games **display name**. The display name is shown only to you, in the Settings screen of your own device. The player id is stored in your save so that the Game can recognise the linked account. The Game does not receive your Google e-mail address, real name, contacts, friends list or photo.

**Cloud save.** While you are signed in, the Game stores one save file (the data listed in section 3, as plain JSON, together with a short summary and a time stamp) in Google's **Saved Games** service, which keeps it in the hidden app-data area of your Google Drive. Google processes that file under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). We have no server and cannot read your cloud save; only the Game running on a device signed in to your Google account reads and writes it. The Game uploads a few seconds after a change and when you pause or close the app. When your device save and your cloud save differ, the Game shows you both and lets you choose; the one you do not pick is replaced.

**Google's own features.** Google Play Games may create a gamer profile for you and offer its own features (achievements, friends, profile visibility). Those features, and what other people can see of your Play Games profile, are controlled in the Google Play Games app and your Google account settings, not by us. The Game publishes nothing about you.

**Unlinking.** "Unlink Google Account" in Settings makes the Game stop using the account and stops uploads. Your progress stays on the device. The cloud save remains in your Google account until you delete it (Google Play Games settings > Delete Play Games data for this Game, or Google Drive > Settings > Manage apps > hidden app data), or until you use Reset progress while signed in, which uploads an empty save in its place.

Google Play Games sign-in and cloud save are available on Android. If we offer a comparable feature on another platform, we will describe it here first.

## 5. Purchases and payments

The Game sells gem packs and two subscriptions (weekly and monthly) through the store's in-app purchase system: Google Play Billing on Android (through the Unity In-App Purchasing library on your device) and, where the Game is offered there, Apple's in-app purchase system.

- **Payment data.** Payment is processed entirely by Google or Apple. We never receive card, bank, billing-address or payment-account details.
- **What the Game receives on your device.** For a completed purchase the store software gives the Game the product id, the store order or transaction id, and a purchase token or receipt that the Game uses to verify the purchase before it grants the gems or subscription. The Game stores the product id, kind, transaction id and time in your save (section 3). The receipt itself is not kept by the Game beyond the purchase. The Game also reads the store's localised price text so that prices can be shown in your currency.
- **Restoring purchases.** "Restore" in Settings asks the store software on your device for your purchase history for this Game and re-grants active subscription days. This reads data from your store account on your device; it is not sent to us.
- **What we receive from the store.** Google Play and Apple provide developers with sales and subscription reports and order details (for example order id, country, amount, currency, tax and refund status) under their own policies. We use these reports only for accounting, tax, refund handling and fraud prevention, and keep them as long as tax and accounting laws require. We cannot identify you from them unless you contact us about an order.
- **Unity.** The Unity In-App Purchasing library is made by Unity Technologies. It may process device and transaction information to run the purchase, as described in the [Unity Privacy Policy](https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy).

Purchases, refunds and subscription management are governed by the [Google Play Terms of Service](https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), or by Apple's media services terms and the [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## 6. Advertising

The Game is free to download and is partly funded by **rewarded video ads**. An ad plays only after you tap an ad button (for example "watch an ad to revive"), and you can always decline. There are no banner ads and no ads that interrupt play uninvited.

**Who serves the ads.** Ads are delivered by **Unity LevelPlay**, an ad mediation platform operated by ironSource Ltd. and Unity Technologies, and by the advertising networks that LevelPlay mediates. At the date above the Game is configured for the LevelPlay (ironSource and Unity Ads) network. If we add further ad networks we will list them here before they go live.

**Data the ad software processes.** To request, show and measure an ad, the LevelPlay software on your device and the ad networks it connects to may process:

- your device's **advertising ID** (Google Advertising ID on Android; the Game declares the `AD_ID` permission for this purpose), and, where applicable, other resettable device identifiers;
- your **IP address**, from which an approximate location (country or city level) can be derived;
- **device and app information:** device model, operating system and version, language, screen size, network type, app id and version, and SDK version;
- **ad events:** which placement requested an ad, impressions, clicks, video completion, and the reward signal that tells the Game you watched the ad to the end;
- **diagnostic data** about ad loading and display errors.

This data is used to deliver ads, to limit how often you see the same ad, to detect fraud, to measure performance, to pay the Game for completed views and, only where you have consented, to personalise the ads you see.

**Personalised and non-personalised ads.** The Game requests ads as **non-personalised** by default. Where the law requires consent before personalised advertising (for example the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the Game shows a consent prompt before the first ad and passes your answer to the ad software; you can change your answer later through the same prompt where it is offered. Non-personalised ads still use the advertising ID for frequency capping and fraud prevention. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads); if you delete it, the ad software receives a string of zeros instead.

**Ad content.** The ads themselves come from third-party advertisers. Tapping an ad can open a third-party website or store page outside the Game, where that party's privacy policy applies.

The processing by Unity and ironSource is described in the [Unity Privacy Policy](https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy) and the [Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy](https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy).

## 8. Data we do not collect

- No account, sign-in, name, e-mail address, phone number or password is required or collected by the Game. Google sign-in is optional and handled by Google.
- No analytics, usage-tracking or crash-reporting software is included, and the Game sends no usage events to us.
- No precise location, contacts, calendar, photos, files, camera or microphone access.
- No chat, messages, friend lists, user-generated content or anything other players can see.
- No push or remote notifications; the Game has no notification feature at all.
- No server of ours: the Game never connects to a computer operated by us. The only network activity is performed by the Google (or Apple), Unity and ad-network software described above.
- No sale of personal data, and no cross-app tracking by us.

If the Game gains a feature that changes any of this (for example leaderboards, events, notifications or analytics), we will update this policy before the feature is released and tell you about it in the Game or in the store release notes.

## 14. Your choices

- **Play as a guest.** Nothing leaves your device except what sections 5 and 6 describe.
- **Sign in or unlink Google** at any time in Settings.
- **Reset progress** in Settings deletes all progress, wallet and items on your device (and, if you are signed in, uploads the empty save to your Google account). Purchase records are kept so that subscriptions can be restored.
- **Ads.** Do not tap ad buttons and no ad is requested or shown. Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. Where a consent prompt is offered, decline or withdraw consent to personalised ads.
- **Purchases.** Manage subscriptions, payment methods and purchase approvals (for example "require authentication for purchases" and Family Link approvals) in your Google Play or Apple account.
- **Haptics, sound, music, reduced motion, text size and left-handed layout** are in Settings and stay on your device.

## 15. Children and age

The Game is a general-audience game. It is **not directed to children under 13**, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13, or under the higher age at which a person can consent to data processing in their country (for example 16 in parts of the European Union). If you are below that age, do not play the Game, or ask a parent or guardian to set it up and supervise your play through their own account.

If you are under 18, you may use the Game only if a parent or guardian has read this policy and our Terms of Use and agrees to them for you. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, a person under 18 is a child and their parent or lawful guardian must give verifiable consent; we do not knowingly track or monitor children's behaviour or serve them targeted advertising. The Game requests ads as non-personalised by default, and personalised ads require an explicit consent that a child cannot give.

Parents and guardians: you can block or require approval for purchases in Google Play (Family Link, purchase authentication) and in Apple's Screen Time and Ask to Buy. If you believe a child has provided personal data to us or to an ad network through the Game, contact jpdash.creator@gmail.com and we will delete anything we hold and help you contact the network concerned.

## 16. Your rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, India DPDP Act, LGPD and others)

Because we hold no server-side data about players, most rights are exercised directly on your device or with Google, Apple or the ad networks. We will still help with any request. In practice:

- **Access and portability:** your progress is on your device and, if signed in, in your Google account (Google Takeout exports Play Games data). We can provide a copy of any order or e-mail record we hold about you.
- **Correction:** edit progress and settings in the Game; order data is corrected through the store.
- **Erasure:** Reset progress, uninstall, and delete the Game's Play Games data (section 13). We will delete e-mail and order records we hold unless tax or accounting law requires us to keep them.
- **Restriction and objection:** you can object to processing based on legitimate interests (section 10) by contacting us; you can stop ad processing by not tapping ad buttons, deleting your advertising ID and withdrawing consent.
- **Withdraw consent:** at any time, through the consent prompt where it is offered, or by e-mail; withdrawal does not affect processing that already happened.
- **Automated decisions:** we do not profile you or make automated decisions with legal or similar effects about you.
- **European Economic Area and United Kingdom:** you may complain to your local data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office).
- **California and other US states (CCPA/CPRA and similar laws):** we do not sell personal information. Personalised advertising by ad networks may count as "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising; you can opt out by declining or withdrawing ad consent where the prompt is offered and by resetting or deleting your advertising ID in Android settings, which also acts as your "Do Not Sell or Share" request for the Game. We do not knowingly sell or share personal information of consumers under 16. You have the right to know, delete and correct, and not to be discriminated against for exercising your rights. An authorised agent may act for you with proof of authority.
- **India (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023):** you have the right to access a summary of personal data processed, to correction and erasure, to grievance redressal, and to nominate another person to exercise your rights. Our grievance officer is J. P. Dash, jpdash.creator@gmail.com. If you are not satisfied with our response you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.
- **Brazil (LGPD):** you have the rights of confirmation, access, correction, anonymisation, portability, deletion, information about sharing, and revocation of consent, and may complain to the Autoridade Nacional de Proteção de Dados.
- **Other countries:** you have whatever rights your local law gives you, and we will honour them.

To exercise a right, e-mail jpdash.creator@gmail.com from the address you want us to reply to and describe your request. We may ask for information that lets us verify you (for example a store order number) before acting on it. We respond within one month, or within the shorter period your local law requires, and we do not charge for requests unless the law allows it for excessive or repeated requests. Data held by Google, Apple, Unity or ad networks is controlled by them; contact them to exercise rights over it, and we will point you to the right place if you ask.

## 17. Security

Your progress is protected by your device's own security (app sandboxing, lock screen and device encryption). The Game's network traffic is performed by Google, Unity and ad-network software over encrypted connections. We hold no player data on servers, which removes the most common breach risk. No method of storage is perfectly secure, so keep your device updated and protected, do not install modified copies of the Game, and report any security concern to jpdash.creator@gmail.com.
