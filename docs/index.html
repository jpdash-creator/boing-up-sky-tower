Thirty regions on one painted map.

Five are open to climb today. Each region has its own look, its own music and its own platform tricks.

Swipe or scroll sideways to see the map pages.

Built to feel fair, and good.

Every jump is fair.

A standing jump always reaches the next platform, and a bot has to finish every level before you ever see it.

Made for one thumb.

Controls sit in the lower half of the screen, and a left-handed layout mirrors them.

Heroes that grow.

Collect fluffy climbers, then merge three of the same hero into a stronger one. Hero powers join your power bar for free.

Easy on your senses.

Reduced motion, sound and haptics switches, and large readable text are built in.

Pick your pace.

Five speeds, an endless tower and a three minute sprint.
