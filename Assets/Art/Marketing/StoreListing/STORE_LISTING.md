## 3. Full description (max 4000; this one is 2709 chars). Paste everything between the lines.
-----
Climb the sky, one bounce at a time!

Meet Momo, a fluffy white climber with a pink scarf, and bounce up towers of floating platforms in a cheerful, hand-painted world. Collect stars and gems, dodge the tricky bits, unlock new regions and gather a whole family of fluffy heroes. Steer with one thumb and jump with the other.

FIVE REGIONS, 25 LEVELS EACH
Climb through Meadow Springs, Mushroom Grove, Sunny Beach, Candy Canyon and Bamboo Temple. Every region has its own look, music, platform tricks and a signature surprise, from bouncy flowers and mushroom caps to drifting clouds, sticky gum and gongs. That is 125 hand-made levels to clear, with up to three stars on each. More regions are on the map, marked "Coming soon".

FAIR AND FUN
Every level is checked by a bot that must be able to finish it, so a tricky tower is never impossible. Tricky platforms never knock you out, and failing and trying again takes a single tap.

PICK YOUR PACE
- Five speeds, from Relaxed to Insane, so the tower climbs as fast as you like.
- Endless Tower: no last floor, just how high you can get. Beat your best floor in every region.
- Time Attack: three minutes on the clock, how many floors can you reach?
- Earn bronze, silver and gold medals by beating the pace on a level.

COLLECT AND MERGE HEROES
- Each region brings its own hero to join you: Momo, Capi, Otto, Bonbon and Bao, and many more fluffy friends such as Bumble, Ribbi, Pip, Pinch, Gummi, Tora and Ryu.
- Heroes come in five tiers: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Elite and Legendary. Merge three copies of a hero to make the next tier.
- Every hero jumps and moves a little differently, and higher tiers carry special hero powers.
- Open Iron and Gold chests for hero cards, coins and power-ups.

POWER-UPS
Fly with Flight, blast off with Rocket, add a Double Jump, cling with Wall Stick, protect yourself with Shield, pull in treasure with Magnet or calm the tower with Slow Time. Pick the right one at the right moment.

REWARDS EVERY DAY
Claim daily and weekly rewards, finish simple goals and earn coins, gems and stars as you climb.

MADE FOR EVERYONE
- Left-handed layout that mirrors the controls
- Reduced motion option
- Haptics, sound and music switches
- Adjustable text size and large, readable buttons
- Big, easy-to-hit buttons: left and right to steer, one to jump
- Play as a guest, or sign in with Google Play Games to keep a cloud save

FREE TO PLAY
Fluffy Climbers: Tower Jump is free to download. It shows optional rewarded video ads that you only watch when you choose to, and offers optional in-app purchases (gem packs and subscriptions) that include randomised chest rewards.

Questions or feedback? Write to jpdash.creator@gmail.com.
